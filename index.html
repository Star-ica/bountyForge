How It Works

From bounty to payout — fully on-chain

Every step is enforced by smart contracts on BOT Chain. No middlemen, no delayed payments, no disputes without recourse.

01
🏢
Organization Posts

Register your org, define a bounty with technical requirements, set the reward and deadline.

02
🔒
Funds Enter Escrow

Deposit BOT or USDT directly into the smart contract. Funds are locked until approval — no one can touch them arbitrarily.

03
👩‍💻
Developers Submit

Browse open bounties, attach your GitHub repo and PR link, and submit your work on-chain.

04
✅
Review & Approve

The organization reviews submissions and approves winners. Rejected devs can raise disputes resolved by admin.

05
💸
Rewards Paid Out

Approved developers withdraw rewards directly from the contract. Payment is instant and transparent on BOT Chain.
